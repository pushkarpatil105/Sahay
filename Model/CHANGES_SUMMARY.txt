================================================================================
NEW FEATURES
================================================================================

1. Corridor Overlap Rejection
   - Routes represented as spatial grid-cell corridors
   - Jaccard similarity computed between routes
   - Routes exceeding overlap threshold (50%) are rejected
   - Only geographically distinct corridors survive

2. Forced Midpoint Diversification
   - Generates 8 perturbed waypoint anchors (N, S, E, W, NE, NW, SE, SW)
   - Injects waypoints into Google Routes API requests
   - Forces Google to generate distinct corridors
   - Perturbation distance: ~3km

3. Diversity-Constrained Route Fetching
   - Iteratively searches for geographically unique routes
   - Rejects overlapping corridors in real-time
   - Continues until sufficient diversity found
   - Gracefully falls back to synthetic routes

4. Route Uniqueness Evaluation
   - Evaluates geographic uniqueness of routes
   - Filters routes by minimum overlap threshold
   - Batch evaluation for multiple routes

5. Diagnostics & Monitoring
   - corridor_overlap_score: Overlap with other routes
   - unique_corridor_ratio: Percentage of unique cells
   - scan_diversity_score: Route scanning diversity
   - route_geographic_uniqueness_score: Overall uniqueness
